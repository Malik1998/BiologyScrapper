How to score

  • What FLR measures: tissue laxity only, on a firm-to-flaccid spectrum - it does not try to estimate age. Score what you see in the photo, not how old the person looks.
  • 4 regions, 1 sign each: upper face → eyelid folds, middle face → nasojugal folds, lower face → jowls, neck → neck profile.
  • Angle matters: a downward gaze or off-angle head tilt can make jowls/neck profile look worse than they are. If a photo is angled, keep that in mind rather than scoring the angle itself.
  • Quality matters: a blurry or overexposed photo can wash out real signs of laxity and lead to a falsely low score.
  • When in doubt, use NA: if a sign genuinely isn't judgeable in this particular photo (bad angle, blur, occlusion), pick "NA - not scoreable" rather than guessing.
  • Use the comments box for anything else worth flagging, e.g. "too distorted to score reliably."
